Story & Philosophy

Engineering at the intersection of circuits and code.

I'm Dhyan Krishna M, an embedded systems and web developer focused on creating functional, intelligent Internet of Things (IoT) hardware accompanied by clean, reactive web interfaces.

Whether wiring sensor arrays, flashing firmware onto ESP32 microcontrollers, or designing real-time telemetry dashboards using Chart.js and Tailwind CSS, I love creating practical solutions that solve everyday challenges.

My work ranges from environmental monitoring with BME280 sensors and automated relay control systems to static audio web players connected to real-time APIs. I believe hardware is at its best when it provides seamless, accessible visibility to the person using it.

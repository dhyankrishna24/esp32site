Presentation Masterclass: 4 Keys to a 10/10 Seminar

Recommended guidelines when presenting slides in classrooms, conferences, or college seminars.

01

The 1-Minute Rule

Allocate an average of 1 to 1.5 minutes per slide. A 15-minute seminar should have roughly 10-15 content slides.

02

Visuals Over Walls of Text

Avoid full paragraphs. Use architecture diagrams, bullet takeaways, and clear schematics to guide your audience.

03

Anchor Problem & Solution

Start by framing the real-world engineering challenge within 60 seconds before diving into theoretical details.

04

Mastering Q&A

If you don't know an answer, acknowledge it transparently and explain how you would scientifically investigate it.

How to Add Your PPTs & Seminar Slides

Super simple 2-step setup to host and showcase your presentations.

1

Place your slide file in the folder

Save your PowerPoint (.pptx) or PDF document into the slides/ directory. You can also use a public Google Drive or OneDrive link.

2

Add the entry in slides-data.js

Open slides-data.js in your project folder and append this block: 

{
  id: "my-topic",
  title: "My Presentation Title",
  category: "Computer Science",
  type: "PPTX",
  slidesCount: "25 Slides",
  academicLevel: "College Seminar",
  author: "Your Name",
  date: "2026",
  summary: "Brief overview of what the presentation covers...",
  topicsCovered: ["Point 1", "Point 2", "Point 3"],
  fileUrl: "slides/my_presentation.pptx"
}
3

Instant Deployment

Refresh your page or push to GitHub Pages! Your presentation will appear with instant search, category filtering, and preview.

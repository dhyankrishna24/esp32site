    Terms of Service

    1. Purpose and Educational Use

    EduSphere is an open academic repository created for student learning, classroom seminars, and technical reference. All presentations, formula summaries, and interactive utilities are provided free of charge for non-commercial educational purposes.

    2. Academic Integrity and Attribution

    Users who utilize slide decks or cite material from EduSphere are expected to adhere to academic honor codes and appropriately cite original authors using the provided APA or IEEE bibliographic formats.

    3. Student Submissions and Content Rights

    When contributing presentation materials or study flashcards, you confirm that you hold the right to share the content or that the material is distributed under fair use / open academic licensing. Do not upload copyrighted material without permission.

    4. Disclaimer of Warranties

    The platform, calculation solvers, and telemetry endpoints are provided as-is without warranty of any kind. Technical calculations (such as Ohm's Law or Big-O approximations) are intended for educational estimation and should be independently verified for critical engineering designs.

    Privacy Policy

    1. Client-Side Data Architecture

    EduSphere operates purely as a client-side static application. We do not maintain user account databases, do not collect personal identities, and do not track individual browsing habits across sessions.

    2. Local Storage Usage

    Your browser's local storage (HTML5 localStorage) is used strictly for functional preferences: saving your color theme preference (light or dark), storing presentation bookmark IDs, and persisting custom flashcards you author. This data remains on your physical device and is never transmitted to external marketing servers.

    3. Third-Party Trackers and Analytics

    We do not load third-party behavioral analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or telemetry beacons. External links (such as Google Drive or GitHub) are subject to their respective platform privacy terms.

    4. Contact

    If you have questions regarding data handling or wish to request removal of a student presentation, contact the maintainer at anupuknambiar@gmail.com.
